## Exercise images and animations — third party, ownership unresolved

The 180×180 thumbnails and the animated GIFs are **not** covered by the MIT license above.
Their ownership is currently **unresolved**, and two parties make claims that contradict
each other:

- The upstream dataset attributes them to **© [Gym visual](https://gymvisual.com/)**
  ([terms](https://gymvisual.com/content/3-terms-and-conditions-of-use)), redistributed there
  with that rights holder's written permission — a permission granted to *that dataset* and
  **not transferable**.
- **ExerciseDB/AscendAPI** describes itself as the original creator and owner of this content
  and publishes its own [terms](https://exercisedb.io/faq), which permit self-hosting, bundling
  and commercial display, while prohibiting redistribution of the raw dataset or media as a
  standalone or competing content package.

**This repository does not contain the media.** It is not in the tree, not in the history, and
not in any build output. The database stores only filenames (`exercises.image_path`,
`exercises.gif_path`), and
[`scripts/sync-exercise-media.sh`](scripts/sync-exercise-media.sh) copies the files from the
upstream source into the deployment's own `exercise-media` Supabase Storage bucket at setup time.

**Running that script mirrors third-party content into storage you control.** If you intend to
publish or ship the result — commercially or not — clear it with the rights holder first, and
keep any attribution that accompanies it intact.

The analysis of the provenance above follows
[openGym](https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym)'s own `NOTICE.md`, which documents the same
dataset and the same contradiction.
